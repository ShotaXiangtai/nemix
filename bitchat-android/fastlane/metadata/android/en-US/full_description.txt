nemix is a serverless, peer-to-peer, end-to-end encrypted messenger.

WORKS OFFLINE
Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Aware mesh networking lets you message nearby people directly, even without cellular service or in airplane mode.

NO SERVERS
There are no developer-run servers. No account and no phone number are required.

PRIVACY
- End-to-end encrypted messages, images, voice messages and calls
- EXIF metadata (location, device info) is stripped from images before sending
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking
- Screenshot protection and delete-for-everyone

FEATURES
- Contact exchange by QR code or link, including expiring links
- Encrypted P2P groups in stream or channel format
- Share images, voice messages and location
- Encrypted voice calls
- Optional internet messaging through public Nostr relays
